## Information we process

- **Account information:** your email address, username, account identifiers, account status, and account creation information. We receive the password you submit for authentication and store a password hash rather than the plaintext password.
- **Your content:** workspace names, tasks, descriptions, priorities, status, dates, tags, links, uploaded file contents, and related records. File metadata includes its original filename, attachment name, type, size, storage identifier, uploader, status, and creation time. Files may also contain metadata supplied with the file.
- **Authentication and security information:** login-session records, hashed refresh credentials, password-reset challenges and attempt counts, and abuse-prevention identifiers. Network requests necessarily disclose an IP address to the systems receiving them. The app uses derived identifiers for rate limiting; hosting and storage providers may separately maintain request logs.
- **Messages you send us:** your contact details, the contents of support, privacy, or abuse reports, and information needed to respond. Sending a report to the contact address also involves the email services you and we use.
- **Browser settings:** the theme and font you choose and the credentials needed to maintain your sign-in. See [Browser storage](/legal/storage).

For your safety, we recommend not storing passwords for other services, payment-card details, government ID documents, health records, or other highly sensitive information in tasks or attachments. This is data-minimization advice, not a new blanket prohibition on lawful sensitive topics. Do not upload another person's personal information unless you have the necessary authority and a lawful basis to do so, and limit it to what your task needs.

An email address, username, and password are required to create an account. Without them we cannot register you or provide authenticated workspaces. Task and attachment content is supplied at your choice; not uploading a file does not prevent using other task features.

We use information to create and secure accounts; store, display, update, and delete content as you request; provide file transfers; deliver account and password-recovery messages; answer requests; investigate abuse; comply with applicable law; and establish or defend legal claims.

Where a law requires a legal basis, providing the service generally supports processing necessary for our agreement with you; protecting the service and responding to abuse may support legitimate interests, subject to your rights; responding to binding legal duties may rely on legal obligation. Where consent is required, we must obtain it for the specific processing before it begins, and you may withdraw it. Acknowledging this notice is not blanket consent for unrelated uses.

## Who receives information

The planned production service at donee.dev uses these providers for the stated roles:

- **Cloudflare:** frontend hosting and delivery; receives browser requests, IP addresses, and related request or security information. Its edge processing is not described as confined to one region.
- **Railway:** backend hosting; processes account, task, authentication, attachment metadata, and API request information needed to run Donee.
- **Neon PostgreSQL:** database hosting; stores account records, password hashes, task/workspace data, attachment metadata, and authentication records.
- **AWS S3:** attachment storage in the US East (Ohio) region, us-east-2; processes uploaded file contents, object metadata, and storage requests.
- **Mailgun:** transactional account and recovery email; processes recipient addresses, message contents, and delivery events.
- **Support email:** the public @gmail.com contact uses Google Gmail to receive sender details and report contents. If the contact provider changes, this disclosure must be updated along with the address.

These are the selected providers, not confirmation of a completed production audit. Cloudflare's product and settings, other provider regions, logs, recovery history, retention, contracts, and any additional subprocessors remain pending verification. A US storage region does not establish where every provider handles support information or metadata.

Workspaces, tasks, and files are intended to be private to the account owning the workspace; collaboration and public sharing are not launch features. Files are transferred directly between your browser and storage using temporary signed addresses. Anyone who obtains a valid download address may be able to use it until it expires, so treat it as confidential. Normal use does not publish your tasks or files in a public directory. The service is not end-to-end encrypted: authorized operational access may be possible for support, security, legal compliance, and service delivery.

We may disclose information when reasonably necessary and legally permitted to investigate violations, protect safety, respond to valid legal process, or comply with reporting and preservation duties. We limit disclosures to what is appropriate for the purpose. If ownership of Donee changes, any transfer of personal information must remain subject to applicable law and required notice.

We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. Nothing in the Terms transfers ownership of your content to Donee.

## Retention and deletion

We keep account and content records while they are needed to provide your account and service, subject to deletion requests and legal requirements. You can delete supported tasks, attachments, and workspaces in the interface. Deleting a workspace does not itself delete your account. A failed file-cleanup operation may require a retry.

There is currently no self-service account-deletion or complete account-export control. Email us to request access, correction, a copy, or deletion. We may need to verify your identity without asking for your password. Establishment and end-to-end verification of the manual process remain pending; publication of this notice does not confirm that this work is complete.

Our intention is prompt permanent deletion from active service systems after an authorized deletion, without a voluntary recovery grace period. Receiving a request, verifying it, executing deletion, and confirming completion are separate steps. A failed storage cleanup is not completed deletion.

Active-system deletion does not by itself erase Neon recovery history, S3 noncurrent versions or replicas, caches, provider logs, delivery records, or support correspondence. Their production settings and expiry periods remain pending verification. We have not disabled recovery safeguards merely to make a zero-retention claim.

Authentication credentials expire, but expiry does not mean every associated record is immediately erased. Limited acceptance evidence, privacy-request records, abuse decisions, or legally required preservation may need separate retention with restricted access. Their minimum necessary retention criteria, release procedures, and the account-deletion completion schedule remain pending confirmation. This policy makes no promise of immediate erasure everywhere or a fixed backup-deletion deadline.
