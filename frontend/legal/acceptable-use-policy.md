> Effective October 26, 2026. Last revised October 26, 2026. Version 2026-10-26.

- Upload, store, link to, solicit, or distribute child sexual abuse material, sexual exploitation material, trafficking-related content, or content promoting exploitation of a child.
- Store or distribute non-consensual intimate imagery, sexual extortion material, or unlawfully created or shared intimate deepfakes.
- Infringe copyright, trademarks, trade secrets, privacy, or other rights. Having a copy of a file is not necessarily permission to upload or distribute it.
- Use the service for threats, stalking, harassment, doxxing, fraud, impersonation, or unlawful discrimination; facilitate violence, terrorism, or other crimes.
- Upload malware, phishing content, stolen credentials, malicious scripts, or files intended to compromise another user or system.
- Access, test, scrape, alter, or disclose another person's private data without authorization; bypass permissions, rate limits, suspensions, or file restrictions; or deliberately disrupt or overload the service.
- Use Donee as a public file host, malware distribution point, or a way to evade another service's lawful restrictions.
- Submit abusive or knowingly false reports to silence others, obtain their personal information, or cause wrongful removal.

Lawful reporting, research, criticism, and other legally protected uses are not automatically violations simply because a topic is sensitive. However, that does not authorize possession or distribution of material that is itself illegal. Contact us with identifiers rather than uploading a suspected illegal file to demonstrate a problem.
